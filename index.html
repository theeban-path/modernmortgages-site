FAQ

Common questions.

Does it cost anything to work with a mortgage agent?

For most residential mortgages with qualified borrowers, there is no fee to you: the lender pays the broker's compensation. Some private, alternative or commercial files may involve a fee; if so, it is disclosed in writing upfront before you commit.

What is the minimum down payment in Canada?

5% of the first $500,000 of the purchase price and 10% of the portion between $500,000 and $1,499,999. Homes priced at $1.5 million or more need at least 20% down. With less than 20% down, mortgage default insurance (e.g. CMHC) is required and the premium is added to your mortgage.

What is the mortgage stress test?

Lenders must qualify you at the greater of your contract rate plus 2% or 5.25%. It doesn't change your actual rate or payment, but it does limit how much you can borrow. The Affordability calculator above applies it automatically.

When should I start shopping my mortgage renewal?

About 120 to 150 days before your maturity date. Most lenders will hold a rate for up to 120 days, and switching lenders at renewal is often possible without a penalty.

Can I get a mortgage if I'm self-employed?

Yes. Depending on how long you've been in business and how you report income, options include traditional lenders using two years of tax returns, stated-income programs with B lenders, and private lending. I match your file to the lender whose income rules fit.

Can you help if my credit isn't perfect?

Yes. Alternative (B) lenders and private lenders consider files that the big banks decline, such as bruised credit, recent consumer proposals or non-traditional income. The goal is usually a short-term solution and a plan to move you back to a prime lender.
